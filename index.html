How it works

The 3 things your contract is hiding.

We don't summarize. We surface the clauses that work against you — with the specific statute that says it's allowed or not.

🚩 Red flags with teeth

Security deposit traps. Lease break penalties. Arbitration waivers. Hidden auto-renewals. We name the clause, explain what it does, and quote the law.

"Cal. Civ. Code §1950.5 limits deposits to 1× rent for unfurnished units."

📊 Compared to what's normal

How does your lease stack up against the typical agreement in your state? We tell you if your late fee is above the legal cap, if your deposit is too high, if your landlord is asking for things they can't legally ask for.

Late fees > 6% of rent are unenforceable in 11 US states.

FAQ

The questions people ask before signing up.

Is this legal advice?

No. Plainly is an information tool, not a substitute for a lawyer. It tells you what the law says so you can have a more informed conversation with your landlord, employer, or attorney. We are not your lawyer, and we don't create an attorney-client relationship.

What kinds of contracts can it read?

Right now: residential leases (US, UK, CA), and SaaS / freelance MSAs. Coming next: job offers, NDAs, medical bills, gym memberships. If you can paste it as text, we can read it.

How accurate is it?

Every flag is grounded in a specific statute we cite by section number. We mark uncertainty as uncertainty — if a clause is in a grey area, we say so instead of inventing a confident wrong answer. v0.1 is in beta; expect occasional misses.

Is my contract private?

Yes. We don't store the documents you upload after the analysis completes. We don't train on them. We don't share them with third parties. The Telegram bot runs end-to-end on this principle.

How much will it cost?

Target: $5/month, unlimited reviews. Free during the v0.1 beta. We'll email you before we flip the switch.

What if my jurisdiction isn't covered?

We're starting with US (CA, NY, TX done; FL, IL, WA in progress), England & Wales, and Canada (Ontario, BC, Alberta). Tell us your jurisdiction on the waitlist form — we prioritize based on demand.
